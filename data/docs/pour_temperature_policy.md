# Pour Temperature Policy

The approved pour temperature range for the aluminium alloy is 700 to 730 degrees Celsius.

If the pour temperature falls below 700 degrees Celsius for more than 15 minutes, stop the pour and notify the maintenance team. Low pour temperature is the most common cause of cold shut and porosity defects.

Furnace thermocouples must be calibrated every month. A drifting thermocouple should be replaced.
