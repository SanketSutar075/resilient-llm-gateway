# Casting Defects SOP

Porosity: acceptable reject rate is up to 2% of parts per shift. Above 2%, the shift supervisor must log the batch and check degassing.

Cold shut: caused mainly by low pour temperature or slow fill. Any cold shut on a safety-critical part is an immediate reject.

Shrinkage: check riser size and cooling rate. Repeated shrinkage on the same mould means the mould must be inspected before the next pour.
